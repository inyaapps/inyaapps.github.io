Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 9, 2026

Inya Apps ("we", "our", or "us") develops and publishes mobile applications and related digital services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use our applications.

This policy applies to applications published by Inya Apps that link to this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the application and features you use, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Mobile number
  • Email address
  • Account information
  • Information provided by the user
  • Device information
  • Android version
  • App version
  • IP address
  • Advertising identifiers
  • App usage and interaction information
  • Crash and diagnostic information

The information collected depends on the specific application's features.

2. How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Provide and operate our applications
  • Create and manage accounts
  • Provide requested services and features
  • Improve our applications
  • Analyze application usage and performance
  • Provide customer support
  • Detect fraud, abuse, and security issues
  • Send important notifications
  • Fix technical problems
  • Comply with applicable laws

3. Analytics

Our applications may use services such as Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, or similar analytics services to understand application usage and improve our services.

These services may collect information about device type, app version, app interactions, and usage events.

4. Advertising

Some Inya Apps applications may display advertisements.

Where advertising services are used, third-party advertising providers may collect certain device and advertising-related information according to their respective privacy policies.

5. Sharing of Information

We may share information when necessary with:

  • Service providers
  • Analytics providers
  • Advertising providers
  • Cloud and hosting providers
  • Payment providers, where applicable
  • Government or legal authorities when required by law

We do not sell users' personal information as a general practice.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss, or misuse.

However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as necessary to provide our services, fulfill legitimate business purposes, resolve disputes, maintain security, and comply with legal obligations.

8. Account and Data Deletion

Where an application provides account creation, users may request deletion of their account and associated personal information through the deletion options provided in the application or by contacting us.

Some information may be retained where required or permitted by law.

9. Children's Privacy

Our applications are not generally intended for children unless specifically stated otherwise.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

10. Third-Party Services

Our applications may use third-party services such as Google Play Services, Firebase, Google Analytics, advertising networks, authentication providers, payment providers, and other services required for application functionality.

These third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

11. App Permissions

Depending on the application's functionality, we may request permissions such as:

  • Internet
  • Notifications
  • Camera
  • Photos and files
  • Location
  • Contacts
  • Microphone

Permissions are requested only when required for the relevant functionality.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date on this page.
